# Settings

## Order

<img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/wiki/bornaradusin/WallpaperChanger/images/settings-2.png" width="260" alt="Order and image position">

| Setting | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Random wallpaper order** | Nothing comes back until everything has been shown once. Off: in order. |
| **Include subfolders** | Also uses what is in folders inside the added folders. Folders whose name starts with a dot are skipped. |
| **Automatically rescan added folders** | Off, or every 1–24 hours, so new files turn up on their own. |

## Image position

| Setting | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Fill** | Fills the screen; the edges that don't fit are cut off. |
| **Fit** | All of it shows, with black bars. |
| **Stretch** | Stretched to the shape of the screen. |
| **None** | Shown at its own size, in the middle. |
| **Scroll with the home screen** | Wide pictures move as you swipe between home screen pages. |
| **Full resolution** | Loads pictures at full size instead of the screen's: sharper when cropped, uses more memory. |
| **Darken wallpaper** | 0–80 %, so the icons and text on the home screen are easier to read. |

## Videos

<img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/wiki/bornaradusin/WallpaperChanger/images/settings-3.png" width="260" alt="Videos and lock screen">

- **Loop**: plays over and over while you can see it, and pauses when you can't.
- **Play once**: plays from the start each time the wallpaper comes into view, then stops on its last frame.

This is the setting for all videos. Each video can have its own: see [[Albums]].

## Weather effects

Rain, storms with lightning, snow, fog, clouds, sun by day and stars at night, drawn gently over your wallpaper as the weather is where you are. From 1.4.0.

| Setting | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Both** | On the home and lock screens (the default). |
| **Home** | On the home screen only. |
| **Lock** | On the lock screen only. |
| **Off** | No weather over the wallpaper. |

The card also shows the weather now, where, and when it was last checked, or why it couldn't get it. **Check the weather now** fetches it straight away.

**Last time your wallpaper was on screen** says what the wallpaper did: showing the real weather, showing the test, or why nothing showed (no weather yet, the phone counted as locked, and so on).

**Test mode**: while it's on, the wallpaper shows the effect you pick (rain, storm, snow, fog, clouds, or sun and stars) whatever the weather, and the settings say so in red. Go to the home screen to see it, then turn Test mode off for the real weather again.

Finding where you are: the phone's last known location, else one quick location from Wi-Fi and mobile masts (never GPS), else an approximate one from your internet connection.

The weather goes by what is actually falling where you are, so a shower shows as rain even when the forecast only calls it cloudy.

**Battery:** the weather comes from [Open-Meteo](https://open-meteo.com) at most every half hour, and only while the wallpaper is showing. It uses the location the phone already has from other apps and never turns on GPS itself; without location permission it goes by an approximate location from your internet connection. **Use my location** asks for permission, and to follow you while the phone is locked too, set the app's Location to **Allow all the time** (**Open location settings**). The animation stops whenever the wallpaper can't be seen.
