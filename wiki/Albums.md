# Albums

Albums keep your wallpapers in sets, like one for winter and one for summer.

- The **switch** on each album decides whether the rotation uses it. With all of them off, everything is used. When the lock screen has its own rotation, each album has a **Home** and a **Lock** switch instead (see [[Lock screen]]).
- **⋮** renames or deletes an album. Deleting only removes it from the app; your photos stay on the phone.
- **Add new album** at the bottom.

## In an album

<img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/wiki/bornaradusin/WallpaperChanger/images/album.png" width="260" alt="An album"> <img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/wiki/bornaradusin/WallpaperChanger/images/picture.png" width="260" alt="A picture's options">


- **Add pictures/videos** picks single files; **Add folder** adds a whole folder. Folders are looked through again on their own (see [[Settings]]) and with **Scan folders now**.
- Tap a picture for **Set as wallpaper now** (or **Set for the home screen now** / **Set for the lock screen now**), **Crop**, or **Delete from the album**.
- Long-press to select several, then **Delete**.
- **Now** marks what's on the wallpaper (**Home** and **Lock** when the lock screen has its own).

## Crop

<img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/wiki/bornaradusin/WallpaperChanger/images/crop.png" width="260" alt="Crop">

Drag the frame (the shape of your screen) over the part you want, set its size with the slider, then **Save**. **No crop** shows the whole picture again. Works for videos too.
