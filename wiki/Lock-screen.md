# Lock screen

Normally the lock screen shows the same as the home screen.

On Android 14 and newer, the lock screen can have **its own pictures and videos**, from its own albums and folders:

1. Settings → **Lock screen** → turn on **Lock screen has its own pictures and videos**.
2. Tap **Open the wallpaper picker** and set WallpaperChanger choosing **Home screen**. Then do it again choosing **Lock screen**. The settings show a ✓ for each screen once it's set.
3. On the Albums tab each album now has a **Home** and a **Lock** switch. Switch on the albums each screen should use. Put the lock screen's folders in their own album and switch only **Lock** on for it.

Each screen keeps its own place in the rotation and its own shuffle, and both move on at each lock. Rules that switch album switch it for both screens.

For one fixed lock screen picture (or video), use an album with just that one in it.
