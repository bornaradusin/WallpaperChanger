# Getting started

1. Install the APK from the newest release on GitHub.
2. Open WallpaperChanger and tap **Add folder** (a whole folder, e.g. *Pictures/Wallpapers*) or **Add files** (single pictures and videos). You can add as many as you like.
3. Tap **Set as wallpaper**. Android's wallpaper preview opens: tap **Set wallpaper** and pick *Home and lock screen*.
4. Lock the phone. When you turn the screen on, the next one is up.

**Next now** puts up the next one straight away, without locking.

## What counts

Any picture (JPG, PNG, WebP, HEIC…) or video (MP4, WebM…) the phone can open. Pictures and videos are cropped to fill the screen, never stretched. Videos are always muted.

## New files in a folder

Folders are looked through again every 30 minutes (at the next lock), so new files turn up on their own. **Look through folders again** does it now.

## Colours

Android's Material You colours, and launchers that follow the wallpaper (like TheLauncher), change with each picture or video.
