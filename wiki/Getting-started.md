# Getting started

1. Install the APK from the newest release on GitHub.
2. Open WallpaperChanger. There's an album called **My wallpapers** ready. Open it and tap **Add pictures/videos** or **Add folder**.
3. Tap **Set as wallpaper** (at the top of the app until it's set). Android's preview opens: tap **Set wallpaper**, for the home screen and lock screen.
4. Lock and unlock the phone: the next one is up.

**Next** on the Albums tab puts up the next one straight away.

<img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/wiki/bornaradusin/WallpaperChanger/images/albums.png" width="260" alt="Albums tab">

## What counts

Any picture (JPG, PNG, WebP, HEIC…) or video (MP4, WebM…) the phone can open. Videos are always muted.

You can also share pictures and videos from the gallery to WallpaperChanger and pick the album they go into. They are copied into the app, so they stay even if the gallery's copy goes.

## Colours

Android's Material You colours, and launchers that follow the wallpaper (like TheLauncher), change with each picture or video.
