# Widget, tile and shortcuts

## Widget

Add the WallpaperChanger widget to the home screen. When it's added you choose:

- **Action**: Next wallpaper, Next album, Select wallpaper (pick from a grid), Open current image (in the gallery), or Pause/Resume rotation.
- **Widget text** and **Text color**.
- **Widget icon** on or off.

Long-press the widget and choose its settings to change them later.

## Quick settings tile

Edit the quick settings and add **Wallpaper rotation**. Tap it to pause or resume.

## Shortcuts

Long-press the WallpaperChanger icon for **Next wallpaper**, **Next album** and **Pause/Resume rotation**. Drag one to the home screen to keep it there.
