# WallpaperChanger

A live wallpaper for Android that puts up a **new picture or video each time you lock the phone**, like Wallpaper Changer, with videos as well. The lock screen can have its own pictures and videos.

Like Wallpaper Changer, it changes a second after the screen goes off, once Android has finished locking. The new one, and the phone's colours to match, are ready when you wake the phone, and nothing stutters.
